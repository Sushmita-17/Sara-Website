Category A: Food
Seeds
Chia seed
Chia seeds are tiny powerhouses of fiber, omega-3s, protein, and minerals. They significantly benefit digestion, weight management, heart health, blood sugar control, and bone strength. Remember to soak them before eating for safe and effective consumption.
Pumpkin seed
Pumpkin seeds are a nutritious source of protein, healthy fats, fiber, and vital minerals like magnesium and zinc. They offer benefits for heart health, sleep quality, prostate function, and blood sugar control. Enjoy them raw, roasted, or as a versatile addition to many dishes.
Quinoa seed
Quinoa is a powerful, complete protein seed packed with fiber, essential minerals, and antioxidants. Naturally gluten-free, it's excellent for digestion, blood sugar control, weight management, and heart health. Just rinse before cooking and enjoy this staple.
Sunflower seed
Sunflower seeds are small, nutty powerhouses packed with healthy fats, Vitamin E, and vital minerals like selenium and magnesium. They offer great benefits for heart health, immunity, and reducing inflammation. Enjoy them raw, roasted, or as a crunchy addition to meals
Flax seed
Flax seeds are tiny, golden or brown seeds prized for their high content of omega-3 fatty acids (ALA), fiber, and lignans (powerful antioxidants). These nutritional powerhouses are particularly beneficial for heart health, digestion, and hormone balance. For optimal absorption, it's best to consume them ground, easily added to smoothies, oatmeal, or baked goods.
Basil seed
Basil seeds, often called Sabja or Tukmaria, are tiny, black seeds from the sweet basil plant. They are highly absorbent, forming a gel-like coating when soaked. These seeds are a good source of fiber, minerals (like calcium and magnesium), and antioxidants. They are known for promoting digestive health (aiding constipation and acidity), aiding in weight management by promoting fullness, and offering a natural cooling effect. Always soak them in water before consumption.
Watermelon seed
Watermelon seeds are edible, black or white seeds found within the popular fruit. Often discarded, they are surprisingly rich in protein, healthy fats (including omega-6s), and essential minerals like magnesium, iron, and zinc. These tiny seeds can contribute to heart health, immunity, and blood sugar regulation. They can be eaten raw, roasted, or added to various dishes.
Powder
Moringa powder
Moringa powder, derived from the dried leaves of the "miracle tree," is a nutrient-dense superfood. It's packed with vitamins (A, C, E, and K), minerals (iron, calcium, and magnesium), protein, and potent antioxidants. Known for boosting immunity, reducing inflammation, supporting energy, and aiding overall wellness, it's easily added to smoothies, teas, or meals.
Gymnema Sylvestre powder
Gymnema Sylvestre powder, derived from a woody climbing plant, is traditionally known as the "sugar destroyer." It's primarily valued for its ability to reduce sugar cravings by dulling sweet taste receptors and for its potential to help lower blood sugar levels. Rich in beneficial compounds, it also offers support for insulin production, weight management, and overall metabolic health.
Milk thistle powder
Milk thistle powder, derived from the Silybum marianum plant, is primarily known for its active compound, silymarin, a powerful antioxidant. It's most celebrated for supporting liver health by protecting cells from damage, promoting regeneration, and reducing inflammation. Beyond liver benefits, it may also help with blood sugar regulation and provide general antioxidant protection.
Jamun Seed powder
Jamun seed powder, derived from the seeds of the Indian blackberry (Jamun fruit), is an Ayurvedic staple known for its potent health benefits. It's rich in antioxidants, fiber, and unique compounds like jamboline and jambosine. Primarily, it's celebrated for its ability to help manage blood sugar levels by improving insulin sensitivity and slowing starch-to-sugar conversion. It also supports digestive health, boosts immunity, and offers liver and kidney protection.
Maca powder
Maca powder comes from the root of the Maca plant, a cruciferous vegetable native to the Andes. It's renowned as an adaptogen, helping the body cope with stress, and is primarily sought after for its potential to boost energy and stamina, enhance libido and sexual function, improve mood, and support hormonal balance, particularly during menopause. Rich in nutrients, it's easily added to drinks and food.
Isabgol
Isabgol powder, also known as psyllium husk, is a highly fibrous natural remedy derived from the seeds of the Plantago ovata plant. It's primarily used as a gentle, bulk-forming laxative for constipation due to its ability to absorb water and create a gel. Beyond that, it helps regulate blood sugar and cholesterol levels, aids in weight management by promoting fullness, and can even firm up stools for diarrhea relief. Always mix it with plenty of water before consuming.
Mucuna powder
Mucuna powder, derived from the velvet bean plant (Mucuna pruriens), is best known for its high natural content of L-Dopa, a precursor to dopamine. This makes it popular for supporting mood, cognitive function, and motor skills. It's also traditionally used to boost energy, enhance libido and male fertility, and reduce stress.
Ashwagandha powder
Ashwagandha powder, from the root of the "Indian Ginseng" plant, is a revered adaptogen in Ayurvedic medicine. It's primarily known for its remarkable ability to reduce stress and anxiety by helping the body manage cortisol levels. Beyond stress relief, it also supports improved sleep quality, boosts energy and stamina, enhances cognitive function, and strengthens the immune system.
Asparagus powder
Asparagus powder, typically derived from Asparagus racemosus (Shatavari), is an Ayurvedic herb celebrated for its adaptogenic properties. It's rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. It primarily supports hormonal balance (especially for women), boosts immunity, aids digestion, and helps reduce stress and anxiety. It's a versatile supplement for overall well-being.
Spirulina powder
Spirulina powder is a blue-green algae renowned as a nutrient-dense superfood. It's packed with complete protein, vitamins (B, K), minerals (iron, copper), and powerful antioxidants like phycocyanin. It's highly valued for boosting immunity, enhancing energy, supporting detoxification, and potentially lowering cholesterol and blood pressure.
Arjuna powder
Arjuna powder, sourced from the bark of the Terminalia arjuna tree, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine, primarily revered as a cardiac tonic. It's rich in antioxidants and unique compounds that help to strengthen heart muscles, regulate blood pressure, lower cholesterol, and improve circulation. Beyond heart health, it also offers benefits for reducing inflammation and supporting overall cardiovascular wellness.
Beet root powder
Beetroot powder, made from dried and ground beets, is a vibrant superfood. It's especially rich in dietary nitrates, which the body converts to nitric oxide, helping to lower blood pressure and improve blood flow. It also contains potent antioxidants (like betalains) and beneficial minerals, supporting athletic performance, reducing inflammation, and boosting overall cardiovascular and cellular health.
Amala Powder
Amala powder, derived from the Indian gooseberry (Amla fruit), is a powerhouse in Ayurvedic medicine. It's exceptionally rich in Vitamin C, antioxidants, and anti-inflammatory compounds. It's highly valued for boosting immunity, promoting healthy hair and skin, aiding digestion, supporting liver function, and balancing all three doshas for overall well-being.
Aleovera powder
Aloe vera powder, made from dried aloe vera leaves, retains many of the plant's beneficial compounds. It's highly valued for soothing and hydrating skin, promoting wound healing, and calming irritation due to its anti-inflammatory properties. When consumed, it can also support digestive health and boost immunity. Always remember to dilute it before use, especially for internal consumption.
Hibiscus power
Hibiscus powder, made from the dried petals of the hibiscus flower, is a vibrant superfood. It's rich in antioxidants (like anthocyanins), Vitamin C, and minerals. Valued for its potential to lower blood pressure, support heart health, aid digestion, and boost immunity, it's also popular in natural hair and skin care. Its tart, fruity flavor makes it a refreshing addition to drinks and food.
Licorice powder
Licorice powder, from the Glycyrrhiza glabra plant, is a sweet herb rich in glycyrrhizin, a powerful antioxidant. It's widely used to soothe digestive issues like heartburn, alleviate coughs and sore throats, and support liver health. Its anti-inflammatory properties also make it popular for skin conditions and overall well-being.
Senna powder
Senna powder, derived from the leaves or pods of the Senna plant, is primarily used as a stimulant laxative for short-term relief of constipation. It works by irritating the bowel lining to promote muscle contractions and bowel movements. It's also used for bowel preparation before medical procedures like colonoscopies. Due to its potent effect, it's crucial to use senna only for short durations to avoid dependence or adverse effects.
Giloy powder
Giloy powder, from the "Amrita" or "Guduchi" plant, is a revered adaptogen in Ayurveda. It's a powerful immunity booster, celebrated for its ability to fight fever and infections, regulate blood sugar, and reduce inflammation. It also supports digestive health and stress reduction, making it a versatile tonic for overall well-being.
Saw palmetto powder
Saw palmetto powder, derived from the berries of the saw palmetto palm, is widely known for its potential to support prostate health, particularly in men with benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) symptoms. It may also help reduce hair loss and balance hormone levels. Rich in fatty acids and plant sterols, it offers anti-inflammatory benefits and can be taken as a supplement for overall wellness.
Baheda powder
Baheda powder, derived from the Bibhitaki fruit, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine and a key ingredient in Triphala. It's highly valued for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, primarily benefiting respiratory health (coughs, colds), digestion (constipation, bloating), and boosting immunity. It also contributes to healthy hair and skin and can help regulate metabolism.
Brahmi Powder
Brahmi powder, from the revered Bacopa monnieri plant, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine known as a brain tonic. It's primarily celebrated for its ability to enhance memory, focus, and cognitive function. Beyond boosting mental clarity, Brahmi also acts as an adaptogen, reducing stress and anxiety, and possesses antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties for overall well-being.
Triphala powder
Triphala powder is a foundational Ayurvedic blend, meaning "three fruits," consisting of Amalaki (Indian Gooseberry), Bibhitaki, and Haritaki. This powerful combination is a celebrated digestive tonic, known for gently cleansing the colon, promoting regularity, and aiding detoxification. Rich in antioxidants and Vitamin C, it also supports immunity, balances all three doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), and contributes to overall well-being.
Harad powder
Harad powder, derived from the Haritaki fruit, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine, often called the "King of Medicines." It's highly valued for its rejuvenating properties and its profound impact on digestive health, acting as a gentle laxative to relieve constipation while also aiding detoxification. Rich in antioxidants, it supports immunity, improves gut health, and contributes to overall longevity and well-being. It's also one of the three fruits in Triphala.
Stevia powder
Stevia powder, extracted from the leaves of the Stevia plant, is a natural, zero-calorie sweetener that's significantly sweeter than sugar. It's widely used as a healthy sugar alternative, particularly beneficial for managing blood sugar levels (ideal for diabetics), aiding weight loss, and supporting heart health. It provides sweetness without contributing carbohydrates or calories, making it a popular choice for sweetening beverages and foods.
Turmeric powder
Turmeric powder, derived from the dried root of the Curcuma longa plant, is a vibrant spice and powerful superfood. Its main active compound, curcumin, provides potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It's widely celebrated for supporting joint health, boosting immunity, aiding digestion, and promoting healthy skin. It’s warm, earthy flavor is a staple in many cuisines and wellness practices.
Ritha powder
Ritha powder, from the "soapnut" fruit, is a natural cleanser traditionally used in Ayurveda. It's rich in saponins, which create a gentle lather, making it highly valued for cleansing hair (reducing dandruff, promoting shine) and skin. It also offers benefits for digestive health and has antioxidant properties.
Shikakai powder
Shikakai powder, derived from the fruit pods of the Acacia concinna tree, is a natural, soap-free cleanser. It's rich in saponins, vitamins (A, C, D, E, K), and antioxidants, making it an excellent natural choice for hair care. It gently cleanses the scalp without stripping natural oils, promotes hair growth, reduces dandruff, and adds shine and softness to hair.
Protein powder
Protein powder is a concentrated source of protein, typically derived from whey, casein, soy, pea, or rice, designed to supplement dietary protein intake. It's primarily used to support muscle growth and repair, aid in weight management by promoting satiety, and ensure adequate protein intake for overall health, especially for athletes or those with higher protein needs. It's easily mixed into shakes, smoothies, or food.
Jaggery powder
Jaggery powder is an unrefined natural sweetener made from concentrated sugarcane juice or palm sap. Unlike refined sugar, it retains minerals like iron, magnesium, and potassium, and offers a distinct caramel-like flavor. It's often used as a healthier sugar alternative, known for supporting digestion, boosting energy, and aiding detoxification, making it a popular choice in traditional cuisines and wellness.
Brown sugar
Brown sugar is a sucrose product with a distinct brown color and moist texture due to its molasses content. It's primarily used as a sweetener in baking and cooking, adding a rich, caramel-like flavor and moisture to dishes. While it contains trace amounts of minerals from molasses, its nutritional benefits are minimal compared to refined white sugar; its main value lies in its flavor and textural contribution to food.

Essential Oil
Lemon grass oil
Lemongrass is an aromatic, tall grass prized for its distinctive lemony, citrusy flavor and aroma. It's widely used in Asian cuisine for soups, curries, and teas, and its essential oil is valued for aromatherapy and natural remedies. It's known for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering benefits for digestion, stress reduction, and overall well-being.
Orange peel oil
Orange peel oil, derived from sweet orange peels via cold-pressing, offers a fresh, sweet citrus aroma largely due to d-Limonene. It's prized in flavoring, fragrances, and aromatherapy, but remember it can cause photosensitivity on skin.
Tea Tree oil
Tea tree oil (Melaleuca alternifolia leaf oil) is a powerful essential oil steam-distilled from the leaves of the Australian tea tree. Known for its distinct, fresh, medicinal aroma and its primary active compound, terpinen-4-ol, it's widely valued for its purifying and cleansing properties, especially in skincare and topical applications. Always dilute before use.
Clove oil
Clove oil is a powerful essential oil steam-distilled primarily from the buds (and sometimes leaves or stems) of the Syzygium aromaticum tree. Characterized by its strong, warm, spicy aroma and high concentration of eugenol, it's widely used for its soothing properties, particularly in dental care and for warmth in topical applications. Due to its potency, always use highly diluted.
Lavender oil
Lavender oil is a beloved essential oil steam-distilled from the flowering tops of Lavandula angustifolia. Renowned for its sweet, floral, herbaceous aroma and rich in compounds like linalool and linalyl acetate, it's extensively used for its calming and soothing properties in aromatherapy, skincare, and promoting relaxation. Generally considered gentle, it's a very popular and versatile oil.
Jojoba oil
Jojoba oil is actually a unique liquid wax ester cold-pressed from the seeds of the Jojoba plant (Simmondsia chinensis). Its molecular structure closely resembles our skin's natural sebum, making it highly non-comedogenic and easily absorbed. Widely prized in skincare and hair care, it's an excellent moisturizer, conditioner, and carrier oil due to its stability and compatibility with all skin types.
Jasmine oil
Jasmine oil is a highly prized absolute, typically solvent-extracted from the delicate flowers of Jasminum grandiflorum or Jasminum sambac. Renowned for its intensely sweet, floral, and exotic aroma, it's widely cherished in perfumery and aromatherapy for its uplifting and mood-enhancing properties, often associated with reducing stress and promoting a sense of well-being. Its extraction process makes it quite precious.
Rosemary oil
Rosemary oil is a stimulating essential oil steam-distilled from the leaves and flowering tops of Rosmarinus officinalis. Known for its strong, herbaceous, and camphorous aroma, it's highly valued for its invigorating properties, particularly in supporting hair and scalp health, enhancing focus, and aiding respiratory comfort. Use with caution due to its potency.

Camphor oil
Camphor oil is a powerful essential oil steam-distilled from the wood of the camphor tree (Cinnamomum camphora). Known for its strong, penetrating, and medicinal aroma, it's widely used topically for its cooling, warming, and decongestant properties, often found in rubs for muscle aches or respiratory issues. Peppermint oil
Peppermint oil is a highly popular essential oil steam-distilled from the leaves and flowering tops of Mentha piperita. Characterized by its strong, fresh, and minty aroma, primarily due to menthol, it's widely used for its invigorating, cooling, and soothing properties, often aiding digestion, alleviating headaches, and promoting alertness. Timmur oil
Timmur oil is a distinctive essential oil steam-distilled from the berries/seeds of the Zanthoxylum armatum plant, native to the Himalayan region of Nepal. It offers a unique citrusy, peppery, and woody aroma with a characteristic tingling or numbing sensation due to its specific compounds. Traditionally used for its local soothing properties and in regional cuisine.
Jatamansi oil

Highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic practices, it possesses a deep, earthy, musky, and woody aroma, renowned for its grounding, calming, and meditative properties, often used to promote tranquility and support hair health.
Medicinal Oil
Natural Hair growth oil
Unlike the single-ingredient oils we've discussed, a natural hair growth oil refers to a formulated blend designed to nourish the scalp and hair. These oils combine nutrient-rich carrier oils (like castor, argan, or jojoba) with specific essential oils (such as rosemary, peppermint, or lavender) known for stimulating follicles, strengthening strands, and promoting a healthy environment for hair growth. Applied topically, their aim is to revitalize hair from root to tip.
Mosquito Repellent oil
A mosquito repellent oil is a formulated blend of natural oils designed to deter mosquitoes and other insects. These blends combine a carrier oil (like coconut or jojoba) with specific essential oils known for their repellent properties, such as citronella, lemongrass, eucalyptus, peppermint, and lavender. Applied to the skin, they offer a natural alternative to synthetic insect repellents.
Pain relief oil
Pain relief oil is a topical blend formulated with various natural oils to help soothe discomfort. It combines carrier oils with potent essential oils like wintergreen, peppermint, clove, or frankincense, known for their warming, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic properties. Applied by massage, it aims to temporarily ease muscle aches, joint stiffness, or localized pain.
Body massage oil
Body massage oil is a specially formulated blend designed to facilitate smooth gliding during massage while nourishing the skin. It combines light to medium carrier oils (like almond, grapeseed and jojoba) for optimal slip and absorption, often infused with essential oils (such as lavender, sandalwood, and chamomile) to provide aromatherapy benefits, promote relaxation, and leave the skin feeling soft and moisturized.
Medicinal Herbs
Giloy 
Giloy (Tinospora cordifolia) is a highly valued medicinal climbing shrub in Ayurvedic tradition, often referred to as "Amrita" (the root of immortality). Primarily, its stem is used, commonly consumed as a juice, decoction, or powder. It's widely recognized for its immunomodulatory and adaptogenic properties, often used to support overall wellness, boost immunity, and help manage various conditions, including fever.
Mint
Mint (various Mentha species, like peppermint and spearmint) is a widely cultivated aromatic perennial herb primarily valued for its leaves. Used fresh, dried, or as a tea, it's a popular natural remedy known for its digestive soothing properties, its ability to freshen breath, and to provide comfort for respiratory issues due to its invigorating aroma.
Gymnema
Gymnema (Gymnema sylvestre) is a woody climbing herb native to the tropical forests of India, Africa, and Australia. Its leaves are highly valued in Ayurvedic medicine, earning it the common name "sugar destroyer" (Gumar in Hindi) due to its unique ability to temporarily suppress the taste of sweetness. It's primarily used to support healthy blood sugar management and to reduce sugar cravings.
Licorice
Licorice (Glycyrrhiza glabra) is a widely recognized perennial herb whose root has been used medicinally for thousands of years. Known for its distinctively sweet flavor (many times sweeter than sugar), it's commonly used in extracts, powders, and teas. It's highly valued for its soothing properties, particularly for the respiratory and digestive systems, and for its traditional use in supporting adrenal health.
Milk thistle
Milk Thistle (Silybum marianum) is a distinctive flowering herb primarily recognized for the medicinal properties found in its seeds. These seeds contain silymarin, a powerful complex of flavonoids. Historically and currently, Milk Thistle is highly valued for its traditional use in supporting liver health, promoting detoxification, and protecting liver cells.
Brahmi
Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri) is a revered creeping perennial herb in Ayurvedic medicine, known for its small white flowers. Primarily, the whole plant is used, often in powders or extracts. It's highly regarded as a nervine tonic and is traditionally utilized for its ability to enhance memory, improve cognitive function, reduce stress, and support overall mental clarity.
Shankhapushpi
Shankhapushpi (Convolvulus pluricaulis is most commonly referred to by this name) is a traditional Ayurvedic herb known for its beautiful, shell-shaped white or blue flowers. Primarily, the whole plant is used in powders or extracts. It is highly valued as a powerful brain tonic, traditionally utilized to enhance memory, improve concentration, reduce mental fatigue, and promote a calm state of mind, making it a key herb for cognitive and nervous system support.
Punarnava
Punarnava (Boerhavia diffusa) is a significant Ayurvedic herb, a spreading perennial plant known for its vibrant roots. Its name, meaning "that which renews the body," reflects its traditional uses. Primarily, the root and whole plant are used in various forms. It is highly valued for its diuretic properties, supporting healthy kidney and urinary tract function, and for its traditional role in promoting liver health and reducing inflammation.
Chirayanta
Chirayata (Swertia chirata) is a highly valued medicinal herb, an erect annual plant native to the Himalayan region. Known for its intensely bitter taste, the whole plant, particularly the dried aerial parts, is traditionally used in decoctions or powders. It is primarily revered in Ayurveda for its fever-reducing properties, its ability to support liver health and detoxification, and its role in improving digestion and purifying the blood.
Neem
Neem (Azadirachta indica) is a revered medicinal tree native to the Indian subcontinent, often called the "village pharmacy" due to its extensive traditional uses. Various parts, including its leaves, bark, and seed oil, are intensely bitter and highly valued. Neem is widely recognized for its powerful purifying, antiseptic, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a staple in Ayurveda for skin health, blood purification, and overall immune support.
Moringa
Moringa (Moringa oleifera) is a highly esteemed medicinal tree, widely celebrated for its exceptional nutritional profile. Often called the "miracle tree," its leaves are particularly rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and amino acids, and are commonly consumed as a powder or fresh. •Apple Ber
Apple Ber (Ziziphus mauritiana), also known as Indian Jujube or Chinese date, is a tropical fruit tree known for its sweet, crisp, apple-like fruit. It is rich in vitamin C, iron, calcium, and antioxidants, making it highly nutritious and beneficial for health. Traditionally, Apple Ber is used to boost immunity, improve digestion, and support respiratory health. The fruit helps in relieving constipation, anemia, and fatigue, while the leaves and bark are used in herbal remedies to treat infections, inflammation, and skin disorders. Its cooling and rejuvenating properties make it a valuable plant in natural medicine systems.
•Dragon Fruit
Dragon Fruit (Hylocereus spp.), also known as Pitaya, is a vibrant tropical fruit that grows on a cactus plant. It is rich in vitamin C, fiber, antioxidants, and essential minerals like calcium and magnesium. Known for its striking appearance and mildly sweet taste, dragon fruit supports immune health, digestion, and skin health. Its high antioxidant content helps fight inflammation and free radical damage, while the fiber aids in maintaining gut health and blood sugar balance. Both the fruit and its seeds contribute to heart health by supporting good cholesterol levels. Dragon fruit is often consumed fresh, in smoothies, or as part of healthy diets.
•Custard Apple
Custard Apple (Annona squamosa), also known as Sitaphal or Sugar Apple, is a tropical fruit known for its sweet, creamy pulp and numerous health benefits. It is rich in vitamin C, vitamin B6, fiber, potassium, and antioxidants, which support immunity, brain health, and digestion. The fruit is traditionally used to boost energy, improve skin health, and support heart function. Its high fiber content aids in relieving constipation, while the seeds and leaves have been used in folk medicine for their anti-parasitic, anti-inflammatory, and antidiabetic properties. Custard apple is both a nutritious and medicinally valuable plant.
•Mango
Mango (Mangifera indica) is a tropical fruit tree widely cherished for its sweet, juicy fruit and valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in vitamins A, C, and E, antioxidants, and dietary fiber, mango supports immune health, eye health, and digestion. In traditional medicine, different parts of the mango tree—leaves, bark, seeds, and flowers—are used to treat ailments such as diabetes, diarrhea, and respiratory issues. Mango leaves are especially known for helping manage blood sugar levels and improving gut health. Both the fruit and tree parts make mango an important plant in both diet and natural healing.
•Litchi
Litchi (Litchi chinensis) is a tropical fruit known for its juicy, aromatic flesh and numerous health benefits. It is rich in vitamin C, antioxidants, and potassium, which help boost immunity, promote healthy skin, and support heart health. Litchi also contains polyphenols that have anti-inflammatory and anti-cancer properties. Traditionally, the fruit, seeds, and peel are used in herbal remedies to treat coughs, fever, stomach issues, and sore throat. With its refreshing taste and medicinal value, litchi is both a delicious and health-promoting fruit.
•Lemon
Lemon (Citrus limon) is a widely used citrus fruit known for its sharp, tangy flavor and powerful medicinal properties. It is an excellent source of vitamin C, antioxidants, and citrates, which help boost immunity, improve skin health, and prevent kidney stones. Lemon is commonly used to aid digestion, promote detoxification, and support weight loss. Its antibacterial and antiviral properties make it effective in treating sore throats, colds, and oral infections. Both the juice and peel are used in traditional remedies and health tonics, making lemon a versatile and beneficial plant in natural medicine.
