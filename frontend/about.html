About Sara Worldwide

Kalanki, Kathmandu, Nepal · Since 2017

Sara Worldwide Business Pvt. Ltd. distributes super foods, organic food, pulses, herbals, fruits and vegetables across Nepal. We source from Himalayan regions and local organic farmers.

Our Objectives

  • Promote natural and organic wellness products
  • Support local farmers and fair trade
  • Provide authentic Himali products (Shilajit, honey, herbs)
  • Deliver quality cosmetics, spiritual items, and nursery plants
